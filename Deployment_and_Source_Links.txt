Suggested walkthrough
-----------------------
1. Sign in as the coordinator, create a shift.
2. Sign out, sign in as the volunteer, sign up for the shift you just created.
3. Sign out, sign back in as the coordinator, open the shift's roster, mark the
   volunteer's attendance.
4. Sign back in as the volunteer to see the "Attended" status reflected.

See docs/TESTING.md for the full test evidence behind this build, and CLAUDE.md for
architecture notes and non-obvious gotchas encountered while building it.
