Suggested walkthrough
-----------------------
1. Sign in as the coordinator (Admin credentials above), create a shift.
2. Sign out, sign in as the volunteer (Test credentials above), sign up for the
   shift you just created.
3. Sign out, sign back in as the coordinator, open the shift's roster, mark the
   volunteer's attendance.
4. Sign back in as the volunteer to see the "Attended" status reflected.
