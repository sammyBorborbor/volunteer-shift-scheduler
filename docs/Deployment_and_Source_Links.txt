Suggested walkthrough
-----------------------
1. Sign in as the coordinator (Admin credentials above) — see the dashboard's
   fill-rate badges across every scenario above without creating anything.
2. Open a shift's roster to see recorded attendance (Senior Center Visit) or an
   active roster (any upcoming shift).
3. Sign in as the volunteer (Test credentials above) and open "My Sign-ups" to
   see every sign-up status in one place.
4. To see the live sign-up/attendance flow end to end: sign in as the coordinator,
   create a new shift; sign out, sign in as the volunteer, sign up for it; sign
   back in as the coordinator, open its roster, mark the volunteer's attendance;
   sign back in as the volunteer to see the "Attended" status reflected.
